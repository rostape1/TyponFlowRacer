## Legs 1–2 — the reaches: level with Wowla, then ahead

- **Same water all the way.** Both boats stayed 55–165 m apart for the whole first reach. Wowla was
  up to about half a minute ahead until the lull at 10:15 (5.4 kn) passed. We moved ahead between
  10:25 and 10:30 when the wind filled to 8–9 kn, and we rounded at 10:37:49, 1.2 min ahead of
  Wowla (10:38:58).
- **On the numbers.** While sailing: 6.4 kn at 69° against a 6.6 kn target (98%) on leg 1, and
  6.5 kn against 6.8 on leg 2. The 91% for leg 1 includes the start and the lull. Current was with
  us (~0.7 kn toward 285°), the same for both boats.

## Leg 3 — first beat: 4.7 min lost side by side, on speed

- **A slow start to the beat.** We rounded at 10:46 onto port and sailed ~2.7 min on port before
  tacking at 10:48:40. Wowla's track heads south straight away. Out of that tack, we sailed
  22–31° to the wind at 3.2–3.4 kn for over a minute, and speed was not back over 5 kn until
  10:50:40. Measured from each boat's rounding, Wowla was 0.14 nm closer to the mark after 5 minutes.
- **Then a long starboard board, 10:50 to 11:08, with Wowla 0.2–0.4 nm to our east (inshore).**
  Over that board Wowla made 4.4 kn toward the wind over the ground and we made 3.8 kn, with Wowla
  sailing 7° higher over the ground (161° vs 154°). By 11:07 we were about 2 minutes behind. The
  wind was steady (180–190°, 8–10 kn), so no shift explains it. The current was setting out of the
  Bay at ~0.6 kn, against us. Wowla, closer to the shore, may have had less of it, or more wind.
  Our instruments can't tell which.
- **Against ORC: 91% while sailing, and all of it was speed.** We sailed 40° through the water
  against a 41° target, so on angle or slightly high. But speed was 5.2 kn against a 5.7 kn target.
  This is the opposite of our usual light-air pattern (too wide). Here we pointed at the target
  without the speed to support it.
- **Underpowered.** Heel was 13° on starboard (heading ~150°) and 10° on port (~226°). The
  difference in helm between tacks gives only about 1° of weather helm. Our logs show 3–6° is fast
  and neutral helm (0–2°) is 0.15–0.3 kn slow.
- **Swell.** Outside the Gate we met a long ocean swell (about 6–7 s between waves, against 2–3 s
  in the Bay). Earlier analysis of this race found that light air in leftover swell is where Typon
  loses and the J/100 doesn't. That was based on few samples, so treat it as a lead.
- **Tacks:** 2 on the leg (11:08:32, 11:14:35). Speed fell to ~3 kn each time and took 47–55 s to
  get back to 95% of its earlier value (the crib-sheet median in 6–10 kn is ~27 s). Wowla rounded
  at 11:22:46 and we rounded at 11:26:09, so Wowla was 3.4 min ahead.

## Leg 4 — run back north: 6° too high, and a slow start

- **The first 3.5 min.** After the bear-away at 11:25:50 we sailed at only 3.4–4.4 kn at 116–144°
  until 11:29:20, and reached 7 kn only at 11:30 as the wind rose to 11–12 kn. In the first 5
  minutes of the leg Wowla made 0.11 nm more than us. This looks like a slow spinnaker set; it
  could also be a soft patch at the mark. Ask the crew.
- **The main board, 11:30–11:50: angle, not speed.** We were at our target speed (6.6 kn against
  6.6) but sailed 142–148° against a target of ~154°. Wowla sailed 148°, and its course over the
  ground was 10° deeper than ours (342° vs 332°). That gave it 6.3 kn of downwind VMG against our
  5.8 kn at the same ground speed. This matches the crib sheet exactly: in 10–13 kn we sail high.
- **A lull at the bottom.** From 11:50 the wind dropped to 7–9 kn. We gybed at 11:53:32 and did the
  last 9 minutes at 4.5–5.5 kn. Wowla rounded at 11:55:47, 6.7 min ahead of us (12:02:32), and
  probably got through before the wind dropped. We can't see its wind.

## Leg 5 — short beat: wide, and slow tacks

- 48° against a 42° target, at target speed (5.4 kn): this time it was **angle**, our usual
  light-air habit. 2 tacks (12:10:00, 12:13:41). Speed fell to 3.3–3.8 kn each time and took
  85–103 s to rebuild.
- From 12:15 to 12:18, both boats sailed at 70–97° to the wind. That looks like an offset leg in the
  course, not overstanding the mark.

## Leg 6 — long run into the Bay: on the numbers

- **99% for both boats.** The wind veered from 206° to 262° between 12:20 and 12:55, so the leg
  started as a broad reach (125–132°) and ended as a run. On the run part we sailed 149° against a
  target of 152°. One gybe at 13:01:43 cost ~6 s. The 0.6 min lost on this leg is all rating.
  Wowla rounded at 13:07:55 and we rounded at 13:16:09, 8.2 min behind.

## Leg 7 — last beat: good pace, lost in the light patch along the city front

- **105% of ORC, and we still lost 5.8 min.** The wind backed from ~265° (13:15–13:29) to
  221–230° (13:35–13:45). That turned much of the beat into a port-tack fetch to the mark, and a
  shift like that scores above 100% against the certificate. Our straight-line pace was good: 44°
  against a 42° target, at 5.7 kn against 5.5 (102%).
- **First 10 minutes: level.** Measured from each boat's rounding, we were the same distance from
  the mark as Wowla (1.87 vs 1.85 nm).
- **Where it went.** On starboard we went further toward the city front than Wowla did. We tacked at
  13:31:56; Wowla tacked ~0.25 nm further out, about 8 minutes earlier. Between 13:31:56 and
  13:46:31 we tacked 5 times in 3.5–7 kn. From 13:41 to 13:46 we got only 0.05 nm closer to the
  mark. Speed bottomed at 2.5 kn after the 13:46:31 tack, and after the 13:43:15 tack it did not
  get back to 95% within 2 minutes. At the same stage of its leg, Wowla was fetching on port at
  6+ kn, 0.2–0.3 nm further from the shore. 30 minutes into the leg we were 0.31 nm behind Wowla's
  progress, and 0.46 nm behind at 35 minutes.
- **The 13:35:23 tack** took us off port, which was lifted and pointing at the mark (heading
  291–296°), onto starboard heading 182°. That was tacking onto the header. We may have been going
  inshore for relief from the current: the flood was ~1.2 kn against us on this leg. Or there was
  a boat or an obstruction. Ask the crew.
- Heel was 14° on starboard and 9° on port, with ~1.5° of weather helm: underpowered again. The gap
  at the last mark was 13.9 min on the water (14:07:41 vs 13:53:46).

## Leg 8 — reach to the finish

- The bear-away at 14:07:30 used 32° of rudder, and speed fell to 2.9 kn. Speed was back to 6 kn
  by 14:08:50. The leg was 86% of ORC against Wowla's 102%, which cost 0.9 min in sailing.

## What we could have done differently

1. **Power up for light air upwind. This is the biggest lever.** On beat 1 we were on angle but
   0.5 kn slow, with neutral helm and 10–13° of heel. On the crib sheet, below ~11 kn: backstay
   off, jib halyard eased, car forward, a fuller main with twist, heel up to ~15°, and 3°+ of
   weather helm. Beat 1 alone cost 4.9 min in sailing.
2. **Groove first, then point. This matters most out of tacks.** At 10:48:40 we sailed 22–31° to
   the wind at 3.2 kn for over a minute. Light-air tacks took 45–100 s to rebuild speed against the
   crib-sheet ~27 s. Twelve tacks on the beats cost minutes, not seconds. In under 7 kn, tack less
   and bear away 2° to build speed before pointing.
3. **Sail deeper on runs in 10–13 kn.** On leg 4 we were at target speed but 6° high, and Wowla was
   10° deeper over the ground: 3.1 min in sailing. The crib sheet's downwind rule: go deeper even
   though speed drops.
4. **Last beat: stay in the breeze on the lifted tack.** When the wind backed, port became the
   fetch. The short tacks along the city front from 13:31 to 13:47 are where most of the 5.7 min on
   the water went. Wowla held port further out.
5. **Faster sets and bear-aways.** 11:25:50 (about 3.5 min under 4.5 kn) and 14:07:30.
6. **Keep doing: the start and the reaches.** We beat Wowla to the second mark by 1.2 min, and the
   long run (leg 6) was on the numbers.

At 99% of our polar over the whole race we would have beaten Final Final, at 103% Wowla, at 106%
Jarlen, and at 109% Feather. We sailed 96%.

## Data limits

- **Wowla's last rounding was matched by hand.** The automatic match failed because Wowla had
  finished before we rounded. Its closest pass of our last mark was at 13:53:46, 1 m away, and
  legs 7 and 8 use that time.
- **Wowla's 106% (beat 1) and 119% (last beat) are not believable as sailing.** Wowla's % uses *our*
  wind and current and AIS every ~30 s. That normally gives ±2–3 points. On legs where it was
  5–10 minutes ahead, or 0.2–0.4 nm away, in patchy, shifting wind and changing current, the error
  is much bigger. Read these figures as "Wowla had better wind or current than we measured". The
  corrected times, and the whole-race 102%, are solid.
- Light-air % is the least certain: ORC's target rises fast with wind, so a 10% wind error moves
  the result ~7 points.
- Feather, Jarlen and Final Final have no AIS, so they appear only in the whole-race split: Feather
  108%, Jarlen 103%, Final Final 99% of their polars. The placing counts only the five boats in the
  results for this race.
- Tack recovery is a simple measure: the time until speed is back to 95% of its value before the
  tack. In shifty 4–7 kn it includes the wind changing.
- No sail-trim data. The slow set at 11:26 and the reasons for the city-front tacks are inferences.
