Numbers are illustrative. Targets come from the ORC cert per TWS (beat angle upwind, gybe angle downwind); the target AWA is ORC's true angle converted at the boat speed you are actually doing.

Mode switches on TWA: upwind under 90°, downwind over. Upwind dial = 0–45° AWA, downwind dial = 60–180° AWA with dead downwind at the top (a broad reach with the kite up reads well forward of 90°). Speed bar spans target ±1 kn: white line = target, fill = your speed (number on the left), dashed = min, the speed floor (target − 0.3 kn).
