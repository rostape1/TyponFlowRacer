## Leg 1 — first beat: the leg that decided the race

- **Where we went.** We tacked onto port at 11:45:59, one minute after the start. Wowla held
  starboard until about 11:48 and went left: from 11:48 to 11:55 it was 600–700 m to our left and
  about 100 m further upwind. It crossed ahead of us by ~150 m at about 11:58. After that it was
  ~300 m to our right when the wind went right (245° at 12:01), which paid it. Wowla rounded at
  12:02:18, we rounded at 12:04:09.
- **Against ORC: 92% while sailing, and it is angle, not speed.** Median 46° through the water
  against ORC's 40°, at 6.24 kn against a 6.21 kn target. We were at target speed, but read ~6°
  wide, most of it on port (see Data limits). The bow-against-bow comparison below is the most
  direct measure of pointing against a rival.
- **Against Wowla: about 2° higher, at the same speed.** Wowla transmits its heading, so the two
  boats can be compared bow against bow and track against track, at the same moments on the same
  tack, within 1 nm, averaging the two tacks (so a difference in the wind where each boat was, or in
  its compass, cancels). On this beat Wowla's track through the water was **1.7° higher**; by the
  bow the two were level (−0.9°), on only 8 + 7 reports. Speeds were about the same (6.2 kn on port,
  6.5–6.6 kn on starboard). A first, cruder look at courses over the ground from 11:48 to 11:55 had
  put Wowla ~5° higher; but Wowla was 600–700 m to our left then, in different wind, and that look
  did not cancel it. At ~1–1.5% of VMG per degree, 2° is ~0.4–0.6 min of this leg. **The rest of the
  1.8 min sailing loss is the route:** Wowla's left, then the right shift at 12:01.
- **By tack against our wind** we read 100% of ORC on starboard (43°) and 90% on port (48°). Part
  of the speed side was the paddlewheel (now corrected); the ~5° wider angle on port is probably
  real, and it recurs on every light-air beat of the series (see Data limits).
- **Heel was fine:** a median of 15–16° on both tacks, never over 25°. That is in the crib-sheet
  range for 10 kn.
- **Tacks:** 4. Back to 95% of speed in 23–61 s (crib sheet 10–13 kn: ~36 s). The slowest was the
  11:55:12 tack onto starboard. Near the top we tacked onto port at 12:01:42, sailed wider (~60°
  at 12:02:30) and tacked back at 12:03:17. That is a short hitch to the mark, not a board.

## Leg 2 — first run: on target while sailing, lost on the route

- **98% of ORC while sailing:** 154.5° against ORC's 155°, and 6.70 kn against 6.73. For the leg as a
  whole it was 91%. We sailed 1.41 nm on a 1.2 nm rhumb line, and the wind swung 247° → 234° → 246°.
  We lost 0.6 min more to Wowla on the water (from 1.9 to 2.4 min behind).
- **Two gybes:** at 12:07:19 (back to 95% of speed in 30 s) and at 12:13:16. After the second gybe we
  were not back to the pre-gybe 7.2 kn for ~100 s. Part of that is probably the puff ending, not the
  gybe; the data cannot separate the two.
- Wowla made 96% of its polar for the leg, and was 70–140 m to our right from 12:07 to 12:12.

## Leg 3 — second beat: even with Wowla, and an untidy top mark

- **Starboard, 12:16:54–12:24:38: a good board.** 45° at 7.2 kn against a 6.5 kn target, heel ~22°.
  The wind veered 237° → 250° (12:18–12:23), which lifted us on
  starboard. Wowla, ~400 m to our right, came back from ~420 m to ~160 m upwind of us between
  12:17:54 and 12:24:54.
- **The tack onto port at 12:24:38 was well timed:** the wind went left again (238° then 232° by 12:26),
  which lifts port.
- **Port, 12:24:38–12:31:04:** 52–55° at 6.4–6.6 kn against a 6.75 kn target. Wowla's upwind lead
  went back to 150–230 m. Wowla's leg was 93% of its polar against our 92%, so at the mark the gap
  was the same as at the first rounding: 2.5 min behind (12:32:15 vs 12:34:43). On this beat the
  two boats were rarely on the same tack at the same time in the shifting wind, so the GPS angle
  comparison of beat 1 cannot be repeated here.
- **Outlier: the top of the beat, 12:30:40–12:34:16.** On port we were already at ~65–70° true
  (apparent ~41°) at 12:30:40, and then we tacked onto starboard at 12:31:04. We sailed 55 s at
  ~5 kn, tacked back at 12:32:00, and fetched the mark on port at ~70° true and 7 kn for over two
  minutes. **That looks like an overstood layline with a double tack in it.** Speed took ~94 s to
  recover from the first of those tacks. We cannot measure the time it cost; it is an inference
  from the angles. (A cross or a duck with another boat would look similar; ask the crew.)
- **Heel:** starboard ~22°, over 25° for 10% of the time. Port ~17°. There was no over-heeling in
  this wind.

## Leg 4 — run to the finish: our best leg

- **97% of ORC for the leg and 103% while sailing:** 164° against ORC's 155°, at the 6.73 kn target.
  Deeper than target, at target speed. This is what the crib sheet asks for downwind.
- Gybes at 12:37:08 and 12:42:52, and a by-the-lee wobble at 12:40:10–12:40:18. Each was back to 95%
  of speed in 20–40 s, except around 12:40–12:41 where the wind also dropped. The breeze built to
  13–14 kn from 12:44 for the finish.
- Wowla made 101% of its polar and gained another 0.3 min on the water.

## What we could have done differently

1. **Beat 1: the start and the first shift decided it.** We tacked onto port a minute after the
   gun while Wowla held starboard to the left; it crossed ahead at ~11:58 and the right shift at
   12:01 paid it again. That is most of the 1.8 min. Then **point ~2° higher**: Wowla's track was
   1.7° higher on beat 1 and 2.7° on beat 2, at the same speed, worth ~0.4–0.6 min a beat. We were at
   ORC's target speed but wider than its angle, so the angle is the lever: build to groove speed
   (6.5 kn at 10 kn), then come up a degree at a time while speed stays above the 6.2 kn floor.
2. **Laylines at the windward mark.** Both beats ended with a short hitch. Beat 3 ended with ~2 min
   fetching at ~70° after a double tack (12:31–12:32). Call the layline once, a little late rather
   than early.
3. **Keep doing: the leg 3 starboard board and the downwind angles.** We went on the lifted tack in the
   12:18–12:23 veer and tacked into the left shift at 12:24:38. The final run was deeper than ORC's
   target at target speed.
4. **The target was reachable.** The whole race was 92% of ORC. To finish 4th (ahead of Jarlen) needed
   about 2.1 min sooner, ~96%. To beat Wowla needed about 4.1 min, ~99%. To win needed ~101%.

## Data limits

- Wowla's % uses *our* wind and current, and AIS every ~30 s, so its per-leg % is ±2–3 points. Its
  rounding matches were good (5–48 m from our rounding points). Its "while sailing" medians on the
  runs (112% and 127%) are not credible and are not used; the leg figures in the table are.
- **Port vs starboard.** We read 100–101% on starboard and ~90% on port on both beats. Part of
  that was the paddlewheel, which reads ~0.4 kn high heeled to port and is now corrected (methodology
  page); an earlier version of this review called the split wind shifts, which it isn't. What is
  left is the angle: under 13 kn port reads ~6° wider on every beat of the series, and our compass
  shows the same bow to bow against Wowla, so it is probably real. The cause is open: the same
  number on the wind display is ~6° wider on port, or something in the rig or sail set-up differs
  by side. Worth checking before the next light-air race.
  The pointing comparison with Wowla uses headings and tracks at the same moments, so it does not
  depend on our wind or boat speed.
- **Pointing against Wowla** rests on few reports on this short race (8 + 7 on beat 1, 7 + 9 on
  beat 2). Wowla's heading sensor is not calibrated like ours; averaging the two tacks removes a
  constant error but not one that changes with heading, so treat ±2°.
- Feather, Jarlen and Final Final have no AIS, so they appear only in the whole-race split (Feather
  100%, Final Final 96%, Jarlen 94% of their polars, against our 92%).
- R2 was sailed entirely inside the Gate in flat water. The Friday "slower outside the Gate" pattern
  does not apply to this race.
- The overstood layline at 12:31–12:34 is inferred from the angles. There is no mark position in
  the data.
- There is no sail-trim data. The logs show *that* we sailed wider than Wowla, not what the trim was.
