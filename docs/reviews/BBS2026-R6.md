## Leg 1 — first beat: the left excursion and the mark approach

- **Where we went.** All three boats started within ~70 m of each other at 11:50. The mark was only
  ~32° off the wind, so port was the long tack. Wowla tacked onto port almost at once and sailed
  near the rhumb line. We and Frequent Flyer held starboard for 6.6 min to 11:56:35 and went about
  1,050 m left of the rhumb line. By 11:56 Wowla was ~800 m closer to the mark. It rounded 2.1 min
  ahead of us on the water (12:18:51 vs 12:20:58).
- **Against Frequent Flyer: same water, lost at both ends.** We were within 40–250 m of each other
  all leg. Frequent Flyer was ~150 m ahead by 11:58, and then stayed ~120–180 m ahead for 20
  minutes. Then it rounded 1.8 min ahead (≈12:19:10). So most of the gap came in the last 400 m.
- **The mark approach: three tacks in 2.7 min.** On the long port tack we were heading 272–292°,
  while the mark bore 266–280°. So we were sailing *below* the mark most of the way, and at 12:17
  it bore 266° against our heading of 292°. We tacked at 12:18:17, tacked back at 12:19:07 after
  only 50 s, and tacked again at 12:20:32 to round. The 12:19:07 tack comes within seconds of
  Frequent Flyer's rounding. That suggests (only an inference) that we had to give way to it or
  keep clear of it.
- **Against ORC: 84%, mostly route and marks.** Against our wind the median angle was 50° through
  the water against ORC's 39.6°, at 6.67 kn against a 6.60 kn target. We read wider on port than on
  starboard, but Frequent Flyer shows the same port/starboard split against our wind (84% vs 102%),
  so that split is a wind-reference bias, not our port-tack sailing (see Data limits).
- **On the water, we pointed with Frequent Flyer.** At the same moments on the same tack, GPS
  courses put Frequent Flyer 2° higher than us on port and 2° lower on starboard. It was 0.26 kn
  *slower* on port. So pace upwind doesn't explain its lead. Wowla's course was 17° higher than ours
  on port, but it was ~1 km away near the rhumb line, pinching up to the mark, and 0.35 kn slower.
  Heading higher on port would have laid the mark and saved the last three tacks.
- **Heel was fine:** median 17° starboard and 18° port. Heel was over 28° for 0–1% of the time.
  The first tack (11:56:35) cost ~33 s. Near the mark, speed was back to 95% within 12–39 s of each
  tack. The crib sheet gives 36 s for 10–13 kn.
- **Current** was mostly with us toward the Gate, ~0.8–1.6 kn by our estimate. The wind was 9–13.6
  kn from 222–247°.

## Leg 2 — reach east at 157°: on the certificate, Frequent Flyer outran it

- **At the certificate's speed.** No gybes. We sailed 157° at a median 7.67 kn, and ORC's speed at
  that angle and wind is 7.55 kn. The leg came out at 97%: the minutes at each end cost the rest.
- **A slow approach to the east mark.** From 13:00 to 13:01, speed fell to 5.2–5.3 kn in 10–12 kn
  of wind, while heading up from 107° to 133°. It looks like the drop and the rounding, but that is
  an inference. It cost less than a minute.
- **Wowla** sailed 159° at 7.79 kn, 100% of its polar for the leg. It rounded 2.0 min ahead, which
  is the same as at mark 1: level on the water, −0.6 min corrected (1.2 sailing, 0.5 back from the
  rating).
- **Frequent Flyer gained 2.8 min on the water.** It went ~800 m further right early (12:24–12:28),
  sailed lower (152°) and faster (8.2 kn median), and rounded 4.6 min ahead (≈12:57:25). That was
  101% of its polar: a Farr 30 on a breezy reach.
- The wind varied 12–17.5 kn, with a lull at 12:52–12:53 (12 kn, 6.5 kn boat speed).

## Leg 3 — second beat: furthest left, no gain, and lower than the rivals on starboard

- **Where we went.** We held starboard to 13:17:38, the furthest left of the three: ~1,450 m left
  of the rhumb line. Frequent Flyer tacked around 13:12 at ~1,280 m left. Wowla stayed 200–550 m
  left. The wind veered to 253–259° from 13:07 to 13:16, which lifted us on starboard. But when we
  crossed back on port we were ~430 m behind Wowla on distance to the mark (13:22), and still ~450
  m behind at 13:30. **The left didn't pay.** On starboard our estimate of the current is 1.2–1.5
  kn setting to ~340°, i.e. across the course, but this is an estimate.
- **Against ORC: 90%.** Against our wind we sailed 45.4° on starboard (19.8 min, 6.66 kn) and 49.6°
  on port (13.2 min, 6.51 kn). ORC's target is 40.0° at 6.38 kn. The port/starboard split is again
  the wind reference: Wowla scores 88% on port against 109% on starboard in the same wind.
- **On the water, 3–5° lower on starboard.** At the same moments on starboard, GPS courses put Wowla
  3° higher than us, 0.18 kn slower; and Frequent Flyer 5° higher, 0.09 kn *faster*. Frequent Flyer
  was higher and no slower: that is the one clear upwind pace deficit in this race. On port the
  boats were rarely on the same tack at the same time (3–6 AIS points), which is too few to judge.
- **The end of the beat.** There were five tacks from 13:30:18 to 13:41:58 along the city front.
  The measured costs were small (−15 to +16 s each; shifts blur the number). Then we sailed the last
  2.1 min at 60–64° true: that looks like an overstand into the mark. Wowla stretched from 2.0 to
  3.8 min ahead (rounding 13:40:16), and Frequent Flyer from 4.6 to 7.0 min (≈13:37:05).
- **Heel was fine again:** medians 17–18°, and over 28° for 0% of the time on either tack.

## Leg 4 — reach to the finish: small

- A 147° reach in 9.8 kn: 6.17 kn against the certificate's 6.49 kn, which is 95%. The first

## What we could have done differently

1. **Point higher once the speed is there, on both tacks.** On beat 2 Frequent Flyer held a course
   5° higher than ours on starboard at the same speed. Against our wind we read 6–12° wide of target
   with speed above target on both beats (how wide exactly depends on our wind reading). Per the
   crib sheet: groove first, then point up a degree at a time while speed stays above the floor.
   Here the speed was already there, so the second step was missing.
2. **Use the heel we had.** Heel was 17–18°, below the crib sheet's 20–25° band. So pointing higher
   had no leeway penalty to pay. This is the opposite of R5.
3. **Get onto the long tack early on a one-tack beat.** With the mark ~32° off the wind, the 6.6
   min of starboard after the start took us 1 km from the rhumb line. Wowla went to port at once and
   led by ~800 m at 11:56.
4. **Lay marks at target angle, not by footing and then tacking.** The three tacks in the last 400 m
   of beat 1 and the 60° finish to beat 2 cost roughly a minute each against Frequent Flyer and
   Wowla (an estimate from their on-water gaps).
5. **Keep doing: the reaches.** Leg 2 was at certificate speed, and the rating on this course was
   neutral (+0.2 min to us over the race). At 95% of ORC we would have been 4th. At 97% we would
   have been 2nd.
