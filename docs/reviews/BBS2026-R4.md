## Leg 1 — first beat: even start, then slightly slower and wider than Wowla

- **Where we went.** At 12:01 we were level with Wowla, ~70 m to windward. Both boats tacked at
  ~12:06 and stayed within ~120 m of each other all leg. **Side of the course was not the
  difference.** Wowla gained ~75 m from 12:03 to 12:06 as the breeze built from ~10.5 to ~13 kn,
  then the gap stayed at 90–110 m. Wowla rounded at 12:13:36, about 40 s ahead of us.
- **Against ORC: 85% for the leg, a mix of angle and speed.** We sailed 42.6° through the water
  against ORC's 39.2°, and 6.34 kn against its 6.63 kn target: ~3° wide and 0.3 kn slow. Off the
  line (12:00–12:04, ~10.5 kn) we sailed 37° at 5.6 kn, which was a good 92% of ORC's VMG.
- **Against Wowla:** Wowla made 6.47 kn against its own 6.36 kn target, which is slightly above
  its polar while sailing.
- **Heel was fine:** starboard median 18.5°, port 20°, rarely past 28°. That is right for 11–12 kn.
- **Tacks:** 2. The 12:06:10 tack took 61 s to get back to 95% speed; the crib sheet's median at
  10–13 kn is 36 s.
- **Overstood the mark.** After the 12:12:26 tack we reached in at 50–80° to the wind for about
  1.5 min. By GPS, we crossed our starboard layline at ~12:11:40, so going past it cost about 20 s.
- **Wind:** 232° at the start, 237–243° from 12:02 to 12:05, a right phase to ~248° around 12:06,
  then ~235° from 12:08. Both boats sailed the same shifts.

## Leg 2 — first run: a slow start, fast port gybe, slow starboard gybe

- **On the water:** Wowla was ~250 m ahead by 12:17, and most of that gain came in the first
  2–3 min after the mark. From 12:15:00 to 12:15:45, just after the bear-away, we sailed 5.5–6.7 kn
  in ~15 kn while Wowla was doing 9 kn. That looks like a slow set, but it is an inference; ask the
  crew. After that the gap stayed at about 250 m.
- **Angle was right, speed depended on the gybe.** We sailed 163° against ORC's 166°. On port gybe
  (12:17:15–12:20:40) we sailed 8.0 kn. On starboard gybe (before 12:17 and 12:20:47–12:23) we
  sailed 6.5–6.8 kn at a similar angle, against a target of ~7.5 kn. GPS speed agrees, so the
  difference is real boat speed, not the speed log.
- **Wowla made 107% of its own polar** (7.9 kn at 162° against a 7.4 kn target). The J/100 was fast
  downwind in 15 kn again, as in R5.
- **Gybes:** 2 (12:17:15 onto port, 12:20:47 onto starboard). After the gybe onto port we were back
  to speed in 16 s. After the gybe onto starboard, speed dropped from 8.5 to ~6.4 kn and did not
  come back before the mark.

## Leg 3 — second beat: split at the leeward mark, then overpowered

- **The rounding was slow.** At 12:23:00 we rounded with the helm hard over (~34°), and boat speed
  fell to 3.5 kn by 12:23:30. From 12:23:10 to 12:26:05 we sailed 38° at 5.6 kn: at target angle,
  but under the 6.1 kn speed floor, for 83% of ORC. Wowla was at 6.7–6.8 kn. The crib sheet says:
  groove speed first, then point.
- **The split.** Wowla tacked onto starboard straight away and went left. We held port to the
  right until 12:26:08, up to ~930 m apart. The wind went left (to ~228° at 12:26), which favoured
  Wowla. We lost about 200 m (from ~40 m behind at 12:23 to ~250 m at 12:28). After that the gap
  held at 140–250 m. Wowla rounded ~12:35:05 and we rounded at 12:36:45, about 1.5 min behind.
- **Against ORC: 80% for the leg, and more speed than angle.** 41.4° against 38.5°, and 6.24 kn
  against 6.93 kn: 3° wide and 0.7 kn slow in 15.4 kn. Wowla sailed 43° at 6.66 kn, 0.15 kn above
  its target.
- **Starboard was overpowered:** median heel 27.7°, past 28° for 46% of the time and past 25° for
  69%, with ~7° of weather helm. From 12:26 to 12:32 heel was 28° and we still made only 87%.
- **The last port tack was the worst stretch of the beat:** 12:32–12:35:40, 47° to the wind at
  6.4 kn, heel 26° (past 28° for 35% of the time), helm ~8°. That is 75% of ORC. It looks like
  footing to hold an overpowered boat, but it could also be sailing for the layline. And we
  overstood again: after the 12:35:43 tack we reached in at 53–90° to the wind, about 30 s lost.
- **Tacks:** 3. 12:26:08 took 67 s to get back to 95% speed and 12:31:59 took 46 s. The crib sheet's
  median in 13–16 kn is 36 s.

## Leg 4 — run to the finish: the biggest loss of the race

- **75% of ORC.** 6.43 kn against a 7.53 kn target at 160° (ORC 166°), in 15.3 kn. The angle was
  fine and the speed was 1.1 kn short. Wowla went from ~300 m ahead at 12:37 to ~600 m at 12:42. In
  the first minutes after its rounding, Wowla sailed 9.3–9.5 kn while we sailed ~7 kn.
- **Starboard gybe again.** 12:37:30–12:41:00: 6.2 kn. Port, 12:41:10–12:44:30: 6.9 kn. Starboard
  again, 12:44:40 to the finish: 6.1 kn in ~16 kn, the slowest stretch of the race (77% of ORC).
  Both gybes were slow to recover: 63 s at 12:41:03 and 88 s at 12:44:35.
- Heel and helm were small on both gybes (heel under 6°, helm under 3°). So nothing in the
  instruments shows a broach or a wipe-out. The loss is steady low speed, which points at the
  spinnaker, the pole or its trim on starboard gybe. The logs cannot see sails; ask the crew.
- Wowla: 8.0 kn at 158° against a 7.5 kn target, 108% of its polar while sailing.

## What we could have done differently

1. **Find the starboard-gybe speed. This is the race.** On both runs, starboard gybe was ~1 kn
   slower than port at the same angle and wind. Matching port-gybe speed on starboard would have
   been worth ~0.8 min on leg 2 and ~0.6 min on leg 4. Sailing leg 4 at leg 2's 87% instead of 75%
   saves ~1.4 min. Check pole height, guy and sheet leads, and how the kite is set up for starboard.
2. **Upwind: groove first, then point.** Both beats were ~3° wide *and* 0.3–0.7 kn slow. After the
   leeward mark we pointed at 38° at 5.6 kn, under the speed floor. Build to 6.4 kn first, then point
   up a degree at a time, and do not go below the floor (6.1–6.2 kn at 14–16 kn). Over the whole
   race, 90% of our polar would have been 4th, 93% (our best sustained in heavy air) 3rd, and 94% a
   tie for 1st.
3. **Keep starboard under ~25° of heel in 15+ kn.** On beat 2 starboard was past 28° for 46% of the
   time, and the last port tack was sailed wide with 8° of helm. Traveller down in every gust past
   25°, backstay on; do not foot off to hold the boat up.
4. **Laylines.** We overstood both windward marks, by ~20 s and ~30 s. Two short races a day give
   little room to make that back.
5. **Roundings and the first minutes after them.** The slow leeward rounding (3.5 kn, then 3 min
   under the speed floor) and the slow start of the first run each cost ground that Wowla kept.
6. **Tacks in 12–15 kn:** 61 s and 67 s back to speed on two of five tacks, against the crib sheet's ~36 s.

## Data limits

- **Leeward mark:** Wowla's AIS has a 94 s gap there (12:21:13–12:22:47), and its closest point to

- **Downwind wind readings differ by gybe:** true wind reads ~2 kn lower on port gybe than on
  starboard in both runs. That makes the per-gybe % unreliable. The boat-speed difference between
  gybes is solid, because GPS agrees with it.
- The wind directions quoted are 2-min averages and move ±5°. Treat the phases, not the degrees.
- The overstanding costs are estimates from GPS and the starboard beat course.
- Feather, Tangaroa, Jarlen, Final Final and Frequent Flyer have no AIS, so they appear only in the
  whole-race split.
- No sail or trim data: the logs show *that* we were slow on starboard gybe, not why.
